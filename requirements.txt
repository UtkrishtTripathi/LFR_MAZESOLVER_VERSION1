Firmware runtime: Raspberry Pi Pico MicroPython (RP2 port).
No pip packages are required by the core firmware.
Optional hardware drivers must be selected and copied to the Pico after verification:
- BNO085 driver compatible with your breakout and MicroPython version
- SSD1306 or SH1106 OLED driver matching your display controller
Desktop-only tests use standard Python 3 and unittest; no NumPy is required.
